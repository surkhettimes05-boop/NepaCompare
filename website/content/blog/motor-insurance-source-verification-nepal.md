Insurance information becomes stale. A reliable comparison should show where a claim came from and when it was reviewed.

## Source hierarchy

1. Nepal Insurance Authority publication or directory
2. Current insurer policy wording or schedule
3. Official insurer product, support or annual-report page
4. Secondary explanation that links back to primary evidence

## Verification checklist

- Does the domain belong to the regulator or insurer?
- Is the insurer currently listed by the Authority?
- Is the document date visible?
- Does the statement apply to the correct product and policy period?
- Is it a marketing summary or controlling wording?
- Can the claim be reproduced without guessing?

Khaacho withholds a plan from organic ranking when its source URL or verification date is missing. Old records should be removed until reviewed again.
