The exact documents depend on the insurer, vehicle and transaction. Shikhar Insurance’s official FAQ identifies a photocopy of the vehicle registration book and a renewal notice from the previous insurer for a no-claim discount as motor-insurance examples.

## A practical preparation list

- Vehicle registration or Blue Book details
- Owner identification requested by the insurer
- Valid driving licence details where relevant
- Previous policy or renewal notice
- Claims history needed for a no-claim discount
- Vehicle make, model, year and permitted use
- Photographs or inspection material requested by the insurer

## Share documents safely

Use only the insurer’s official website, branch or confirmed secure channel. Do not send identification, payment details or full policy documents through social-media messages or an unverified agent link.

## Verify before paying

Confirm the insurer is listed by the Nepal Insurance Authority, check the recipient account or payment page, and retain the proposal, receipt and final policy schedule. A comparison estimate is not proof of cover.
