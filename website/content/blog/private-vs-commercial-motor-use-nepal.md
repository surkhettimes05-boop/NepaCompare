Vehicle use affects the risk an insurer is asked to cover. A private-use proposal should not be assumed to cover delivery, passenger transport, hire or other commercial activity.

Shikhar’s official vehicle page lists damage caused by exceeding stated limitations among its exclusion examples. Siddhartha Premier identifies motor products for private and commercial vehicle categories, reinforcing the need to select the correct use.

## Tell the insurer

- Whether the vehicle carries paying passengers or goods
- Whether it is used for delivery or rideshare work
- Who drives it and how often
- The normal operating area
- Any business-owned or financed interest

## Compare like with like

Two prices are not comparable if one assumes private use and the other commercial use. Ask for written confirmation of permitted use before paying.
