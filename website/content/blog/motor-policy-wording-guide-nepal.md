A policy summary helps orientation, but the issued schedule, wording and endorsements form the contract.

## Read in this order

1. **Schedule:** insured name, vehicle, value, use, period and premium.
2. **Definitions:** special meanings assigned to ordinary words.
3. **Insuring clauses:** the events and losses the insurer agrees to cover.
4. **Exclusions:** events, conduct, property or costs that are not covered.
5. **Conditions:** notification, cooperation, inspection, payment and recovery duties.
6. **Deductibles and depreciation:** amounts or portions retained by the insured.
7. **Endorsements:** additions or changes that may override standard wording.

## Resolve uncertainty before purchase

Ask the insurer to identify the exact wording version and answer material questions in writing. Save the proposal and issued documents. A webpage description may be updated later and should not replace the policy copy.
