Third-party and comprehensive insurance answer different questions. Third-party liability responds when an insured vehicle causes covered bodily injury, death or property damage to someone else. Comprehensive cover adds protection for covered physical damage to the insured vehicle while retaining third-party liability.

## What third-party cover does

Shikhar Insurance’s official vehicle page describes third-party liability insurance as indemnifying a third party when the insured vehicle causes loss of life or property. It also states that third-party liability insurance is compulsory in Nepal. Always confirm the current legal and policy requirements with the insurer or regulator.

## What comprehensive cover adds

The same official source describes comprehensive insurance as responding to accidental physical damage to the vehicle and including third-party bodily injury and property liability. Siddhartha Premier’s product directory likewise identifies comprehensive and third-party options for cars, bikes and commercial vehicles.

“Comprehensive” does not mean every event is covered. Policy exclusions, deductibles, depreciation, permitted use, driver licensing and geographical limits still apply.

## Questions to ask

1. What is the insured value of the vehicle?
2. Which accidental-damage events are included?
3. What deductible or excess applies?
4. How are parts and depreciation handled?
5. Are theft, fire or natural hazards included in the current wording?
6. Who may drive the vehicle and where is cover valid?
7. What is the claims notification process?

## How to choose

Do not choose only by premium. Compare the current wording, exclusions, deductible, claims contacts and the insurer’s final written proposal. NepaCompare summaries are not a substitute for the issued policy.
