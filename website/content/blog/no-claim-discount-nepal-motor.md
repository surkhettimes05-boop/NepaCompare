A no-claim discount recognizes an eligible claim-free period. It is not automatic proof that every insurer will apply the same percentage or calculation.

## Evidence

Shikhar Insurance’s official FAQ lists a renewal notice from the previous insurer as a document used for a motor no-claim discount. Ask your insurer what evidence, time period and policy conditions apply.

## Renewal questions

- Is the discount applied to the whole premium or only a component?
- Does a reported incident affect eligibility?
- Can the history move between insurers?
- What evidence and deadline apply?
- How does a lapse in cover affect the discount?

Avoid publishing or relying on a fixed percentage unless it is supported by the current insurer proposal or regulator tariff. Khaacho does not estimate a no-claim discount without a sourced rule.
