The Nepal Insurance Authority currently displays 14 non-life insurers. That list is the starting point—not a ranking.

## Compare in this order

1. **Regulatory identity:** confirm the company on the Authority’s current list.
2. **Product fit:** verify that the insurer offers the relevant vehicle and use type.
3. **Current wording:** compare coverage, exclusions, deductibles and endorsements.
4. **Claims access:** check official notification channels, forms and grievance contacts.
5. **Service geography:** confirm branches, digital service and repair arrangements relevant to you.
6. **Final price:** compare written insurer proposals on equivalent coverage.

## Avoid weak rankings

Do not choose using an unsourced “claim settlement ratio,” fabricated star score or cheapest-price badge. A useful comparison shows the source and review date for every material claim.
