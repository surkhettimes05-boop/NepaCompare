This guide is insurance preparation, not emergency or legal advice.

## At the scene

Prioritize safety, obtain emergency help and follow police or traffic directions. Photograph damage and surroundings only where safe. Exchange necessary identification and vehicle details without arguing about fault.

## Contact the insurer

Use an official number or branch and record the notification reference. Ask whether the insurer requires a survey, police document, approved repair process or specific claim form before repairs.

## Preserve evidence

Keep photographs, registration and licence copies, the policy schedule, correspondence, estimates, invoices and receipts. Do not alter documents or exaggerate damage.

## Follow the issued policy

Official insurer support pages provide claim forms, but the requirements and deadlines in your own policy control. If there is a dispute, use the insurer grievance route and then the regulator’s published process.
