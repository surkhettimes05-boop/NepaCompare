Renewal is an opportunity to correct details and compare equivalent cover—not just repeat last year’s payment.

## Before requesting proposals

- Confirm vehicle ownership, make, model, year and registration details.
- Update permitted use and regular drivers.
- Gather the previous policy, renewal notice and claims history.
- Review the vehicle value and any finance interest.
- Note changes to accessories or modifications.

Shikhar’s FAQ identifies the registration book and previous insurer renewal notice among documents relevant to motor insurance and no-claim discount review.

## Compare the written offer

Check coverage, exclusions, deductible, depreciation, endorsements, geographical area, policy period, taxes and the final payable amount. Verify the insurer against the Authority’s current list and pay only through an official channel.

## After payment

Confirm that the policy schedule is issued, names and vehicle details are correct, the cover dates are continuous, and claims contacts are saved.
